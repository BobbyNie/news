## OpenAI publishes agentic-work adoption research

- Published: 2026-06-25.
- Source: OpenAI official.
- URL: https://openai.com/index/how-agents-are-transforming-work/
- Credibility: official.
- Summary: OpenAI says agentic AI changes knowledge work from short chatbot interactions to delegated, long-horizon tasks. It reports Codex adoption across OpenAI departments and claims non-developer organizational users grew rapidly, making agentic work a cross-functional workflow topic rather than a developer-only tool.

- URL: https://openai.com/index/openai-broadcom-jalapeno-inference-chip/
